THE CORE LOGIC — THE PHYSICS OF RISK 
Moving from Static Snapshots to Dynamic Trajectories
THE PROBLEM WITH TRADITIONAL SCORING: Traditional credit systems provide a static 
"look-back" score. They tell you where a customer was, but not where they are going. By the 
time a score drops, the customers would have entered a crisis. 
OUR INNOVATION: VECTOR-BASED DETECTION We apply physics-based principles to 7 
behavioral signals to detect a "Financial Death Spiral" weeks before a default occurs. We 
don't just measure debt, we measure the momentum of distress.
●
Risk Velocity (V): Measures the Speed of Change. We track how fast a customer’s 
signals are shifting (e.g., Credit utilization jumping 20% in 15 days). A high-velocity 
change indicates an immediate shift in financial stability.
●
Risk Acceleration (A): Tracks the Intensity of the Spiral. This is the rate at which 
Velocity is increasing. Positive acceleration identifies a "Crisis Spiral"—the moment when 
spending frequency increases as savings decay exponentially.

--- PAGE 9 ---
Signal Framework
Instead of generic population rules, our engine monitors 7 high-frequency behavioral vectors that 
represent the actual rhythm of a customer's financial life. Each signal is measured as a deviation from 
the user’s personal history to detect the earliest signs of distress.
●
Signal 1: Salary Timing (Drift) – Measures the exact day salary arrives against the customer's 
6-month median, delays are the earliest "Look-Ahead" indicators.
●
Signal 2: Income Erosion – Tracks any month-on-month decrease in primary salary amount, 
identifying potential employment instability before it impacts bills.
●
Signal 3: Liquidity Momentum – Monitors the speed of savings depletion; a week-on-week 
drop of >15% signals an exhausting "Safety Net."

--- PAGE 10 ---
Signal Framework
●
Signal 4: Payment Integrity (Bill Drift) – Detects when fixed obligations (utilities, rent) start 
shifting later in the monthly cycle, even if still paid.
●
Signal 5: Failed Auto-Debits – Captures technical or balance-related bounces for EMIs and 
recurring subscriptions, marking the start of "Financial Juggling."
●
Signal 6: Credit Exhaustion – Tracks the Acceleration of credit utilization; rapid jumps toward 
the limit indicate an emergency reliance on debt.
●
Signal 7: Engagement Decay (Digital Silence) – Monitors psychological indicators such as 
avoiding the banking app or a sudden drop in budgeting tool usage.

Data sources and characteristics 
Dataset source: 
1) Bank Account Fraud Dataset Suite (NeurIPS 2022)
2) Home Credit Default Risk
Data Reasoning: 
The Home Credit Default Risk and NeurIPS 2022 Bank Account Fraud (BAF) datasets are 
considered as industry standards due to their robustness in analyzing historical financial capacity 
and real time behavioral anxiety, which are the two key factors in pre delinquency.
Framework used for validating data: CAMELS
The CAMELS framework is an internationally recognized supervisory rating system used by banking 
regulators (like the Federal Reserve and the RBI) to assess the overall condition, performance, and 
risk profile of financial institutions.

--- PAGE 15 ---
Why CAMELS ?
●
Regulatory Rigor : It follows institutional risk-assessment standards
●
Strategic Precision: Instead of sending a generic "Late Payment" notice, a tailored GenAI 
message can be sent based on the specific pillar that is failing (e.g., a "Liquidity" alert vs. a 
"Management" warning).
●
Holistic Risk Discovery: Traditional models often rely on "lagging indicators" (static data). 
CAMELS requires a "qualitative and quantitative" approach that includes Management and 
Sensitivity. This framework justifies why you must add a behavioral dataset (BAF) to a static 
one (Home Credit).

Data Privacy & Minimization
We only store what is truly necessary, the eight key behavioral signals and essential metadata. We 
avoid storing full raw transaction descriptions unless absolutely required. This reduces risk and keeps the 
system focused on meaningful indicators rather than excess data.
●
Purpose Limitation
 
The data collected is used strictly for pre-delinquency intervention. It is not repurposed for 
marketing campaigns or unrelated profiling. The system operates within a clearly defined and ethical 
boundary.
●
Anonymised Data
 
Inside our machine learning pipelines, customer identities are protected. We use hashed 
customer IDs instead of direct personal information. There are no names, phone numbers, or 
identifiable details within the ML layer.
●
Encryption at Rest and In Transit
 
All stored data is secured using AES-256 encryption at rest. For communication between 
services and APIs, we use TLS 1.3 encryption to ensure secure data transfer across the network.
●
Data Retention Policy
 
Risk assessment snapshots are retained only for a defined time period — for example, 24 
months. After that, the data is either automatically archived or permanently deleted according to policy.

Regulatory Compliance (FCA-Aligned Governance)
●
FCA Consumer Duty
 VECTOR is built with strong awareness of FCA expectations, focusing on fair and responsible treatment of 
customers. The system is designed to identify early signs of financial stress and provide proactive support 
before harm occurs. Communication is kept clear, respectful, and empathetic, especially for vulnerable 
customers.
●
GDPR Compliance
 The platform aligns with GDPR principles by ensuring transparency and user control over personal data. 
Customers have the right to understand how decisions are made through explainable AI methods such as 
SHAP. They can request access to their data, obtain explanations for risk assessments, and exercise the 
right to erasure where applicable. A Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is conducted to evaluate and 
manage privacy risks.
●
Model Governance
 Strong governance practices ensure accountability and reliability. Model versions are tracked systematically, 
training datasets are documented, and bias and fairness testing is performed to reduce unintended 
discrimination. The system also follows a defined schedule for periodic recalibration to maintain performance 
and accuracy over time.
●
Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard
 Critical decisions always involve human oversight. High-risk actions require approval from a relationship 
manager, and no autonomous restructuring occurs without review. A clear escalation pathway is defined to 
ensure responsible handling of sensitive situations.
