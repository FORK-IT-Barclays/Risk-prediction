# FAQ 06: How did you "Cluster" or compare unique people?

### ❓ The Question:
If every person is unique, how can the AI compare my data to others? Did we do any clustering?

### 🔬 The Technical Answer:
The AI handles uniqueness through **Segmentation** and **Structural Similarity.**

1.  **Does it use Clusters?** 
    *   The **Universal AI (XGBoost)** does not use KMeans clustering. It uses tree-splitting. However, the **Native Expert Model** DOES use KMeans to group people into "Cohorts" first.
2.  **Pattern Recognition:** 
    *   While your life is unique, your "debt structure" is not. The AI finds "Financial Twins"—thousands of people who shared your same combination of features (e.g., $50k salary, 90% card utilization). 
3.  **Probabilistic Outcome:** 
    *   The AI doesn't say what YOU will do. It says: *"Historically, 80% of people with this exact structural setup defaulted."* It treats you as part of a high-risk population cluster.

**Conclusion:** The AI doesn't need to know who you are; it only needs to know **how your leverage is built.**
