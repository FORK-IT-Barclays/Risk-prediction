## 🚦 Intervention Tiers

### 🔴 Tier 1: RED (Immediate Action)
*Priority: Crisis Mitigation & Capital Protection*
- **Zones:** `EXPONENTIAL_CRASH`, `LINEAR_WORSENING`, `EARLY_VOLATILITY`.
- **Intervention:** Card suspension, mandatory advisor call, debt restructuring offers.
- **Metric:** **Precision Score** (Alert Accuracy).

### 🟡 Tier 2: YELLOW (Intervention Needed)
*Priority: Trend Reversal & Support*
- **Zones:** `REACHING_PEAK`, `RECOVERY_STALL`, `LATE_STABILITY`.
- **Intervention:** Soft limit constrictions, personalized financial coaching, educational nudges.
- **Metric:** **Rehab Velocity** (Time to move to Green).

### 🟢 Tier 3: GREEN (Healthy / Growth)
*Priority: Wallet Share & Loyalty*
- **Zones:** `SOLID_STABILITY`, `LINEAR_RECOVERY`, `TOTAL_REHAB`.
- **Intervention:** Pre-approved limit increases, APR rewards, tier upgrades.
- **Metric:** **LTV Expansion** (Growth in healthy accounts).

## 📊 Performance KPIs

1.  **Intervention ROI**: Savings in potential default losses vs. Cost of intervention.
2.  **Stability Index**: % of portfolio maintained in Tier 3 (Green).
3.  **Default Avoidance Rate**: % of customers who moved from Red/Yellow back to Green.
